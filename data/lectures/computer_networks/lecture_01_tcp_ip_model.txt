# ANU Department of Information Security & Networks
## Course: NET202 - Computer Networks & Communications
### Lecture 1: The TCP/IP Protocol Suite & Transport Layer

#### 1. Layered Architecture Overview
The Internet model consists of five logical layers:
- **Application Layer:** HTTP, HTTPS, DNS, SSH, SMTP.
- **Transport Layer:** End-to-end communication, reliability, multiplexing (TCP, UDP).
- **Network Layer:** Logical addressing and routing across subnets (IPv4, IPv6, ICMP).
- **Data Link Layer:** Framing and MAC address physical hop delivery (Ethernet, Wi-Fi 802.11).
- **Physical Layer:** Transmission of raw bitstreams over copper, fiber, or RF.

#### 2. TCP vs UDP Protocol Comparison
- **Transmission Control Protocol (TCP):** Connection-oriented (3-way handshake SYN/SYN-ACK/ACK), reliable ordered delivery, flow control via sliding window, and congestion control (Tahoe, Reno).
- **User Datagram Protocol (UDP):** Connectionless, lightweight, low-overhead, best-effort delivery without retransmission, ideal for real-time video streaming, DNS lookups, and online gaming.
